## 📊 Use Cases

### **1. Customer Service**
```
Customer: "ሂሳቤ ምን ያህል ነው?" (What is my balance?)
ትሕት: "የእርስዎ ሂساብ ቀሪ ሂሳብ ETB 15,760.25 ነው።" 
      (Your balance is ETB 15,760.25)
```

### **2. Product Information**
```
Customer: "What savings accounts do you offer?"
ትሕት: "We offer several savings accounts:
      - Regular Savings (2.5% interest)
      - Premium Savings (3.5% interest)
      - Children's Savings (4% interest)
      Which would you like to know more about?"
```

### **3. Transaction Assistance**
```
Customer: "I want to transfer money"
ትሕት: "I can help you with that. To transfer money:
      1. Log into mobile banking
      2. Select 'Transfer'
      3. Enter recipient details
      Would you like step-by-step guidance?"
```

### **4. Problem Resolution**
```
Customer: "My card is not working"
ትሕት: "I'm sorry to hear that. Let me help:
      - Is your card blocked or damaged?
      - Have you tried at multiple ATMs?
      For immediate assistance, I can connect you 
      to our card services team."
```

## 💬 Sample Conversations

### **Example 1: Account Balance (Amharic)**

```
User: ሰላም! ሂሳቤን ማየት እፈልጋለሁ
      (Hello! I want to see my account)

ትሕት: ሰላም! በደስታ እርዳዎታለሁ። የእርስዎ የሂሳብ ቁጥር ምንድን ነው?
      (Hello! I'm happy to help. What is your account number?)

User: 1234567890

ትሕት: አመሰግናለሁ። የእርስዎ የሂሳብ ቀሪ ሂሳብ ETB 15,760.25 ነው።
      የቅርብ ግብይት በዛሬ ጠዋት 10:30 ነበር።
      (Thank you. Your account balance is ETB 15,760.25.
      Your last transaction was today at 10:30 AM.)
```

### **Example 2: Loan Inquiry (English)**

```
User: What is my account balance?

      Is there anything else I can help you with?

      1. Personal Loan (up to ETB 500,000)
      2. Home Loan (up to ETB 5,000,000)
      3. Business Loan (up to ETB 2,000,000)
      
      Which type of loan are you interested in?
```
