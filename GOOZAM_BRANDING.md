## 📝 Brand Naming Rationale

### **Why Amharic Names?**

1. **Cultural Relevance**: Resonates with Ethiopian banking customers
2. **Memorable**: Unique, easy to remember
3. **Meaningful**: Each name reflects the product's purpose
4. **Local Pride**: Celebrates Ethiopian language and culture

### **Name Meanings:**

| Product | Amharic | English | Symbolism |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|
| **ትሕት** | Tiht | Respectful/Humble | Courteous AI assistant |
| **ማያ** | Maya | (Wisdom-related) | Intelligence from data |
| **ጋሸ** | Gashe | Shield/Protector | Fraud protection |
